the fourth in a month apparently some people in the comment section say it's the equivalent of ringing a dinner bell the way the spear jabs into the water and the little cloud of blood puffs up from the poor speared fish another comment said that their mate saw a *spearo* get snagged by a great white it had the whole of their legs and midsection in its mouth before it pulled them under like quint at the end of jaws *your bottom half is already dinner while your top half is drowning* I'd say I can't imagine it but I'm imagining it right now if there was a time to have an out of body experience then that would be it the obscene puppet show of a hominid poking out of the jagged cavern of a shark's maw and the little fishy murmurations keeping the necessary distance despite dinner being served and somewhere in the scene there might be an oblivious sea turtle that is really a ferryman for the nautical dead who will take you to the deep dark fissure that the sun sinks into every night giving birth to the blameless pale coin of a new moon
