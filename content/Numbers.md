I'll never be a coder of mathematician but sometimes I get a little bit hynotised by numbers. Like this morning, as I laid in bed after waking up a little too early, I thought about how I was closer to my 80th birthday that I was to my 20th birthday by a couple of weeks. That spooked me a little but then I realised that in five years time, I'll be closer to my 80th birthday than I'll be to my 30th birthday, and even though the maths are simple, it seemed strange to make that ten year jump in five years' time. Then five years after that I'm closer to my 80th than I am to my 40th, then five years after that I'm closer to my 80th than my 60th. Five years after that I'm closer to my 80th than my 70th and five years after that I'm feckin' 80. Five years tick by and a decade falls out behind you. 

It's not necessarily dread that builds up. The final years can also be the best years if the person living them sees them as their best years. Why not?
