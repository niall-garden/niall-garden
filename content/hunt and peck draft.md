I've always been struck by the difference in how pigeons behave when they're on the ground and when they're on the wing. The grounded pigeon is when we see them most and when they're at their most bird brained. Constantly startled, jostling for scraps, defecating and doing that funky, head-bobbing pigeon strut. 

But when they're on the wing, they're so much more graceful and seemingly full of purpose. The air is obviously their element. It's like the ground brings the worst out of them while the air fills them with spirit. 

This doesn't seem like too much of a stretch to assume that they are more vulnerable on the ground from foxes, cats, dogs and even humans, but the ground is where the food is. Their awareness needs to narrow to a hair trigger.
