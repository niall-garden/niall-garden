[Mastodon](https://sunny.garden/@rustyniall)

Mastodon and the rest of the fediverse is our last hope for an open, independent internet. Lots of different accounts across different services that can see and respond to each other. I love Mastodon because it feels like a series of rabbit holes rather than one constant downwards churn of content. Mastodon doesn't make much sense until you find the stuff you love and fill your feed with joy and whimsy. I love it there even though it is my least clouty platform.
