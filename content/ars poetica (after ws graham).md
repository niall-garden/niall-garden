
you see it  
and want others  
to see it too  
and so you send it over  
trusting that the words  
will be able to carry it  
but they never do  
and now the reader  
scrutinises your assemblage  
for hidden things  
that resemble their own hidden things  
and you have already forgotten  
whatever prompted you  
to make it  
in the first place
